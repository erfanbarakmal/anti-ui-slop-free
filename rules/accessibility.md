# Accessibility Rules

- Use semantic HTML.
- Preserve logical heading order.
- Associate labels and controls.
- Ensure keyboard access to interactive functionality.
- Use visible `:focus-visible` treatment.
- Avoid removing focus outlines without a replacement.
- Ensure icon-only controls have accessible names.
- Ensure errors are associated with relevant fields.
- Do not rely on color alone for meaning.
- Maintain sufficient text/background contrast.
- Ensure touch targets are practical on mobile.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` when motion is non-essential.
- Use ARIA to fill semantic gaps, not to replace correct native elements.
